Our Mission

About Willsher Security

Founded in Spring, Texas, Willsher Security LLC was built on a single principle: protection is not a service, it is a responsibility. We serve residential communities, commercial properties, industrial facilities, and private clients throughout Greater Houston with vigilance, discipline, and integrity.

Every officer is vetted, licensed by the Texas Department of Public Safety, and trained to the highest professional standards. We do not cut corners. We do not sleep on watch. And we treat your security as if it were our own family on the line.

From static posts to mobile patrols, from access control to executive protection, Willsher Security stands as the first line of defense between your world and whatever threatens it.

Willie Hodge

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

"From Patrol Officer to Command Staff. Three decades of service, now serving you."

With over 30 years in law enforcement and tactical security operations, Willie Hodge brings command-level expertise to every client engagement. His career spans patrol operations, special tactics, and senior leadership — culminating in the founding of Willsher Security LLC to deliver elite protection standards to the private sector.

Landmark Achievement
Founder of the Harris County Mental Health Unit

Willie Hodge established and led the first dedicated law enforcement mental health crisis response team in Harris County, Texas. This pioneering unit integrated licensed mental health professionals with specially trained tactical officers to de-escalate crisis situations, reduce use-of-force incidents, and connect individuals in crisis with appropriate care rather than incarceration. The model has since been studied and replicated by agencies across Texas and the United States.
